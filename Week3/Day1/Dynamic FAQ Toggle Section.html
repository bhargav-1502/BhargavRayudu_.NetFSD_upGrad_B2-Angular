Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I open a new bank account?
You can open a new account by visiting your nearest branch with valid ID proof and address proof, or apply online through our website.
2. What documents are required for KYC verification?
Government-issued ID proof, address proof, and passport-size photographs are required for KYC verification.
3. How can I reset my online banking password?
Click on the “Forgot Password” option on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password securely.
4. What should I do if my debit card is lost?
Immediately block your card through mobile banking, internet banking, or contact customer support to prevent unauthorized transactions.
5. Are there charges for online fund transfers?
Most online transfers such as NEFT and IMPS are free, but certain services may have minimal charges depending on the transaction type.
